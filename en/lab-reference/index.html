Lab reference

This page collects what you look up again and again while running your lab. The examples use the code mmuster. Replace it everywhere with your own values from /etc/cc-lab.env. The chapters in the last column explain the reasons behind each entry.

The cc-lab.env file

Section titled “The cc-lab.env file”

All three VMs have the same file /etc/cc-lab.env. Scripts and checks read it with source /etc/cc-lab.env, which makes the values available as variables.

VariableMeaningChapter
CC_CODEyour personal code0.3
CC_LANGlanguage of the reports, de or en0.3
CC_FE_HOST, CC_NODE1_HOST, CC_NODE2_HOSTnames of the three VMs, such as mmuster-fe0.3
CC_FE_IP, CC_NODE1_IP, CC_NODE2_IPaddresses of the three VMs in the lab network0.3
CC_GUEST_NETthe internal network cc-net for the VMs of your cloud, a /24 in 172.16.0.0/122.3
CC_GUEST_GATEWAYthe front-end's address in cc-net, ending in .12.3
CC_GUEST_FIRST, CC_GUEST_SIZEfirst address and number of addresses for VMs, from .10 with 200 addresses2.3
CC_VNI, CC_VXLAN_GROUPidentifier and multicast group of the VXLAN2.3
CC_K3S_SERVER_IP, CC_K3S_AGENT_IPfixed addresses of k3s-server and k3s-agent, ending in .110 and .1114.2

Machines and roles

Section titled “Machines and roles”
MachineAddressRole
Front-end mmuster-feCC_FE_IP, plus CC_GUEST_GATEWAY in cc-netOpenNebula, gateway with NAT for cc-net, Gitea, OpenTofu, kubectl, backups
Nodes mmuster-node1 and mmuster-node2CC_NODE1_IP, CC_NODE2_IPKVM and libvirt for the VMs of the cloud
k3s-serverCC_K3S_SERVER_IPcontrol plane with etcd, also runs pods
k3s-agentCC_K3S_AGENT_IPnode for pods, with the advanced tasks of part 5 also for Ollama
Member VMsfrom CC_GUEST_FIRSTleases from cc-net, shown by onevnet show cc-net
Client192.168.210.10 on smartlearnKali Linux with a browser and a terminal, where you work, with your own hypervisor your PC takes this role

From the client you log in to the front-end with ssh mmuster-fe, as set up in chapter 0.4. On smartlearn the client is the Kali VM, with your own hypervisor it is your PC. You reach the VMs in cc-net only from the front-end, for example with ssh root@$CC_K3S_SERVER_IP. On smartlearn the fixed addresses of the three servers are in /etc/netplan/00-eth0.yaml, where the second block from chapter 0.3 put them.

You open a tunnel in the client's terminal, and one connection can carry several -L options. While it stays open, the service answers at http://localhost:<local-port>/ in the client's browser.

To reach Gitea after hardening, forward a free local port to port 3000 of the front-end, for example with -L 3001:localhost:3000. Grafana already takes local port 3000.

Access files

Section titled “Access files”

These files live in your user's home directory on the front-end, and only you may read them. Their content never goes into Git, a screencast or a chat with an AI assistant.

FileContentChapter
~/cc-lab/oneadmin.passpassword of oneadmin for Sunstone2.2
~/.one/one_authuser and password the OpenNebula CLI logs in with2.2
~/cc-lab/tenants/passwords and login files of the group admins3.1
~/platform/tofu/terraform.tfstateOpenTofu state with secret values, kept out of Git4.1
~/.kube/configaddress and certificate for kubectl, full rights on the cluster4.3
~/cc-lab/gitea/password of the user platform and the tokens for Git and Argo CD4.4
~/.git-credentialsthe token Git uses to push to the platform repo4.4
~/cc-lab/grafana.passpassword of admin in Grafana5.1
~/backup/k3s/etcd snapshots, the k3s token and the Sealed Secrets key6.2

The bootstrap script stores the password of admin in Argo CD in a file.

The front-end also has ~/cc with the course repo, ~/platform with your platform repo and ~/cc-reports with the reports of the checks.
