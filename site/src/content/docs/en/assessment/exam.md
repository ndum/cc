## Key facts

| Item | State |
|---|---|
| Weight | 40% of the module grade |
| Length | 60 minutes |
| Kind | Individual open-book exam |
| Aids | All of them, including the internet and AI assistants, but no AI agents |
| Date | Towards the end of the module |

You take the exam on your own. Every aid is allowed except AI agents, that is programs that run commands, change files or reach your lab on their own. You may ask an AI assistant, but you type the commands yourself. Time is short. If you know where to find things in the course and in the documentation of the tools, you get there faster. How the exam counts towards the module grade is described under [Assessment](../).
