In this course you build and run a cloud of your own. On three virtual machines you set up OpenNebula as the infrastructure, split it among tenants, provision a Kubernetes cluster on top of it from code, and monitor everything with Prometheus, Grafana and Loki. A local language model runs as a service on your own platform and helps with operations without sending data anywhere else.

Every chapter follows one case. The cooperative Wolkenwerk Bern runs a shared cloud for its members, among them a medical practice, a fiduciary office, a bike shop with a web shop and a small municipality. Simone Wyss, the managing director, brings a new assignment in every part, and you are the platform team.

Part 0 covers the [course overview](start/overview/), [the lab](start/lab/), your [personal parameters](start/parameters/), [preparing the VMs](start/prepare/) and the [tools and the hand-in repo](start/tools/). The course overview shows part by part what you build in the whole module. [Assessment](assessment/) explains how your grade comes together, and the [glossary](glossary/) explains the terms of the course.
